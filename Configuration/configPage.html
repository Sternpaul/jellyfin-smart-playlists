⚖️ Scoring & Weighting

How a movie's score is built: the base For You score is 0.7 × subcategory-match + 0.3 × mood-match (fixed). On top of that, small nudges are added: your Letterboxd ratings (dominant), TMDB keyword overlap, learned affinity, new-movie recency, novelty, and critical acclaim. Because You Watched ranks by similarity (subcategory 30%, mood 20%, theme 15%, crew 10%, style 10%, era 5%, acclaim 5%, intensity 5%, keywords = KeywordWeight). Turn any weight to 0 to disable it.
